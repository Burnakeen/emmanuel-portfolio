About

I started out editing HTML on pages I had no business touching, just to see what would break. That curiosity turned into a habit of building things properly: semantic markup, CSS that doesn't fight itself, and layouts that don't collapse the moment someone opens them on a phone.

I care about the parts of frontend work that are easy to skip — alt text that actually describes the image, navigation that works with a keyboard, spacing that's consistent instead of guessed. Right now I'm focused on getting better at exactly that: shipping small, complete projects rather than half-finished big ones.

This portfolio is itself one of those projects. No framework, no template — plain HTML and a stylesheet I wrote section by section.
